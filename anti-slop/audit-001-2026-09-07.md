## Findings

### 1. [HIGH — R-25, Hard Gate] Systemic WCAG contrast failures on secondary text — FIXED 2026-09-07

All instances listed below (plus `ChatTransformHero.jsx:95`, an `ink/50`
instance missed in the first pass) bumped to `ink/60` (confirmed
4.5:1-4.57:1, PASS). Decorative icon colors (`TimeSection.jsx:25`,
`Doodles.jsx:25,50`) and the unused, unrendered `CatalogMockup.jsx` were
left as-is since they don't affect a real user. Verified: build clean,
zero horizontal overflow and zero console errors at 375px and 1400px,
Comparison table/cards visually re-checked to confirm row values render
and read clearly against the new color.


Computed actual contrast ratios (not eyeballed) for every `text-ink/NN`
opacity used in the codebase, against both background tokens:

| Class | On `#fff9f2` (paper) | On `#ffffff` (paper-2) | WCAG AA (4.5:1 normal / 3:1 large) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ink/30` | 1.93:1 | 1.93:1 | FAIL (even large text) |
| `ink/35` | 2.18:1 | 2.20:1 | FAIL |
| `ink/40` | 2.49:1 | 2.51:1 | FAIL |
| `ink/45` | 2.86:1 | 2.89:1 | FAIL |
| `ink/50` | 3.31:1 | 3.34:1 | large-text only |
| `ink/60` and up | 4.50:1+ | 4.57:1+ | PASS |

`ink/30` through `ink/45` are used as real, meaningful copy (not
decoration) in:

- `Header.jsx:15` — "Order Disini, dari Studio Harel" (`ink/45`)
- `FinalCTA.jsx:39,47,50` — footer tagline, demo caption, copyright line (`ink/35`, `ink/40`, `ink/30`)
- `Hero.jsx:53` — "Demo interaktif, data contoh, bukan toko asli" (`ink/40`)
- `Comparison.jsx:55,88` — every row label in both mobile and desktop layouts (`ink/40`)
- `PricingTiers.jsx:47,64,106` — price note, retainer line, closing line (`ink/40`)
- `ShippingCalcMockup.jsx:65,70`, `AdminDashboardMockup.jsx:14,40`, `QRISMockup.jsx:8,50`, `CatalogMockup.jsx:27` — mockup micro-copy (`ink/30`, `ink/40`)

This is a design-token-level default (`.font-mono-label` + a low ink
opacity is the site's convention for secondary/meta text), so it repeats
everywhere that pattern is used, not just once. `TimeSection.jsx:25` and
`Doodles.jsx:25`'s `ink/40` are decorative SVG icon color, not text, and
are exempt.

**Fix would be:** bump the floor to `ink/60` (4.5:1, confirmed PASS) for
any of the above that is real copy, or use `text-sm`+`ink/50` only where
the text also qualifies as "large text" (18px+) if the 3:1 tier is
acceptable there.

### 2. [MEDIUM — antislop-copywriting "Negative Parallelism", R-36] Overused "bukan X" contrast construction

The copy defines the product by what it is **not**, repeatedly, across
nearly every section, instead of standing on affirmative description.
Individually each line reads fine; end to end it becomes a formula:

- `TimelineIntro.jsx:15` — "Bukan cuma soal siapa yang punya, ini yang kerasa beda..."
- `content.js:110` (Comparison, Kustomisasi/ordi row) — "...bukan sebaliknya."
- `FAQSection.jsx:7` — "...bukan sewa."
- `FAQSection.jsx:15` — "...bukan beli ulang dari nol."
- `FAQSection.jsx:19` — "...bukan langganan yang bisa diputus sepihak."
- `PricingTiers.jsx:19` — "...bukan beli ulang dari nol." (verbatim duplicate of the FAQ line above)
- `FinalCTA.jsx:16,18` — "Nggak ada form pendaftaran di sini..." + "...bukan yang generik." (two negations in one short paragraph)

**Fix would be:** rewrite 2-3 of the most stacked instances (especially
the FAQ/PricingTiers verbatim duplicate, and the double-negation in
FinalCTA) into affirmative statements. Not proposing to touch all 7,
since some read as natural contrast rather than formula, exact list is
the user's call.

### 3. [LOW/MEDIUM — R-17/R-36, evidence] Unsourced competitor pricing figure used in a financial claim

`content.js` (Comparison "Biaya" row) and `PricingTiers.jsx:94-100` both
state "Sewa platform lain: Rp300.000/bulan / Rp3.600.000/tahun" and derive
"balik modal dibanding sewa cuma dalam ±8 bulan" from it. No source is
cited on the page. This isn't flagged as fabrication (the business owner
likely knows Tokokit's real pricing), but it's a number the whole
Comparison/Pricing argument leans on, worth a quick internal check that
it's still accurate before the WhatsApp blast goes out, since a WA group
is exactly the audience likely to fact-check it against what they've seen
elsewhere.

### 4. [LOW — R-05 Purpose-Gate, informational only] 3-tier pricing with highlighted middle tier

This exact shape ("3 pricing tiers, middle one highlighted as most
popular") is explicitly named in antislop's Part 1 as a common AI-default
pattern. Here it passes the purpose test: `content.js` and `CLAUDE.md`
both confirm these are real, finalized tiers and prices, not a template
filler. No change proposed, noting it only so the reasoning is on record.
